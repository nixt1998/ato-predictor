5.2 General Monitoring Protocol

• ECG Monitoring:
Regular electrocardiogram monitoring with attention to QTc interval changes. Immediate medical attention if QTc >500ms or significant prolongation.
• Electrolyte Management:
Maintain potassium (K⁺) >4.0 mmol/L, magnesium (Mg²⁺) >0.9 mmol/L, and calcium (Ca²⁺) within normal range.
• Symptom Monitoring:
Seek immediate medical attention if experiencing palpitations, chest tightness, chest pain, dizziness, syncope, or dyspnea.
